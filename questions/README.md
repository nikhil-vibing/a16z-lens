# questions/

Drop questions you want answered in the voice of an a16z General Partner. One file per question, named in `kebab-case.md`.

## Quick workflow

Two options:

**Option A — fast** (no file needed): Just say `/ask "your question here"`. The answer comes back in chat (and is auto-archived to `research-output/`).

**Option B — preserved** (when you want to keep the question + your own notes): Copy `template.md` to `<your-question>.md`, fill it in, then say `/ask questions/<your-question>.md`.

## What you get

Answer structure:
1. **Thesis lens** — which a16z thesis the question maps to
2. **The call** — opinionated one-sentence verdict
3. **Why** — 3-5 bullets, each citing a specific partner or precedent
4. **The risk nobody is pricing** — bear case
5. **What would change my mind** — the disconfirming evidence
6. **Closing line** — sharp quotable summation

## What's in scope

- Career strategy as a founder/operator
- Market calls ("is AI agents a real category?")
- Build-vs-buy decisions
- Fundraising strategy
- GTM motion choice
- "Should I go after [X market]?" — thesis-fit questions
- "How do a16z partners think about [Y]?" — worldview questions

## What's out of scope

- Anything requiring real-time portfolio company data we don't have.
- Pure personal advice unrelated to building/investing.
- Questions that need a real lawyer or accountant — the answer will redirect you.
