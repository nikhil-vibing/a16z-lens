# Roblox

## What it does
User-generated games platform. Lets anyone build, share, and monetize 3D games and experiences. Massive base of young users (especially under-16) who create and play games for each other. Often described as a "metaverse" company though Roblox itself uses "human co-experience platform."

## Stage when a16z entered
Multiple rounds; a16z was an early backer.

## Lead partner from a16z
Andrew Chen and the consumer / games practice.

## Thesis bucket it fits
- **Primary:** [[games]] (creator-led platform).
- **Secondary:** [[consumer]] (network effects, social).

## Why a16z backed it
1. **David Baszucki** — patient, technical founder. Worked on the platform for ~15 years before public traction.
2. **Two-sided creator network effects** — creators attract players; players attract creators. Textbook Chen "cold start solved long ago" pattern.
3. **Demographic capture** — owns Gen Z and younger consumers' digital social time.
4. **Platform economics** — Roblox takes a cut of every transaction in the economy. Apple-App-Store-class business model.

## Trajectory / current state
- IPO'd March 2021 (direct listing).
- Daily active users in the 80M+ range.
- $3B+ revenue (TTM).
- Continues to expand into older demographics, branded experiences, AI-generated content.
- Stock has been volatile post-IPO but business remains category-defining.

## Lessons / pattern it sets
1. **Long-arc patience** — Baszucki built Roblox for ~15 years before significant traction. Network effects compound slowly.
2. **Creator-economy + platform economics** is a durable moat.
3. **Owning a demographic** is sometimes more valuable than owning a category.
4. **UGC content scales** — Roblox doesn't make games; it makes the platform games are made on.
