# Stripe

## What it does
Payments infrastructure for the internet. APIs that let any developer accept and move money. Has expanded into: billing, tax (Stripe Tax), banking-as-a-service (Stripe Treasury), corporate cards (Stripe Issuing), fraud prevention (Radar), and a stablecoin/crypto re-entry.

## Stage when a16z entered
Series A and follow-on. a16z has been backing Stripe since the early 2010s.

## Lead partner from a16z
Marc Andreessen (long-running board observer / champion). Multiple a16z partners have engaged with Stripe over the years.

## Thesis bucket it fits
- **Primary:** [[fintech]] (infrastructure layer).
- **Secondary:** [[enterprise-infrastructure]] (developer platform).

## Why a16z backed it
1. **Collison brothers as founders** — Patrick and John Collison were unusual technical+business pairing. Long-term builder energy. Marc has frequently named them as exemplary founders.
2. **Developer-first GTM** — Stripe's "first API call in 7 lines of code" was a wedge against the legacy Authorize.net/Braintree experience.
3. **Massive TAM with no quality incumbent** — payments was a clear "software-eating-the-world" target.
4. **Compounding platform** — each new product (Issuing, Treasury, etc.) widens the moat by deepening the relationship.

## Trajectory / current state (as of 2026)
- Privately valued at $70B+ (after tender / secondary rounds).
- One of the most valuable a16z bets ever.
- Re-entered crypto with stablecoin support (2024-2025) — major aligned-with-thesis move.
- IPO candidate, with timing repeatedly speculated.

## Lessons / pattern it sets
1. **Developer-tools-as-fintech** is a huge wedge — the founder/dev relationship compounds.
2. **The Collison archetype** — young, technical, hyper-literate, patient — has become an a16z founder pattern.
3. **Patience pays in fintech** — Stripe took 15+ years to reach this scale; a16z's willingness to hold is part of the story.
4. **Reputation network effect** — being "the Stripe of X" is itself a Stripe-derived moat.
