# Bio + Health

## The thesis in one paragraph
Biology is crossing from natural science to engineering. CRISPR, AlphaFold, lab automation, synthetic biology, and ML-driven drug discovery have moved biology from "observe-and-describe" to "design-and-build." a16z bio funds companies treating biology as a programmable system: AI-first drug discovery, programmable cells, digital therapeutics, healthcare AI, and the infrastructure layer that makes engineered biology scale. The horizon is decade-plus; capital intensity is high; regulatory mastery is a moat.

## Why now
1. **AlphaFold + structural biology revolution** — protein folding solved; targets that were undruggable are now reachable.
2. **CRISPR + base editing in clinic** — first-in-human gene editing approvals (Casgevy / sickle cell, 2023-2024).
3. **AI/ML maturation for biology** — large biology-specific models (ESM, RFdiffusion, Geneformer).
4. **Lab automation reduces unit cost** — wet-lab cycles compress; experiments become more software-like.
5. **Regulatory acceleration** — FDA's adoption of accelerated paths for breakthrough technologies (still uneven, but the trend is positive).

## Common founder profile
- PhD-level scientific co-founder + operator co-founder.
- AI/ML fluency increasingly required.
- Has run a wet lab AT SCALE before, or has shipped clinical-grade software.
- Knows how to stage milestones for capital efficiency in biotech.

## Anti-patterns
- **"AI for [generic medical thing]"** without a scientifically meaningful wedge.
- **Founders without regulatory plan** — FDA strategy is not optional.
- **Pure-software "digital health" with no clinical-grade evidence path.**
- **Drug discovery plays betting on one molecule** — single-asset biotech is a different game than venture.

## Open questions
1. **AI-drug-discovery: real productivity gains or hype?** — early data is encouraging but most pipelines are still 5-10 years from clinic.
2. **Reimbursement** — even great products fail without payer adoption.
3. **Geopolitical risk** — China bio competition, US BIOSECURE Act, supply chain reshoring.
