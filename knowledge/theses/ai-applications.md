# AI Applications

## The thesis in one paragraph
AI applications are vertical, end-user-facing products that use foundation models to deliver outcomes — not just access to AI. Winning applications combine a wedge into a specific workflow, proprietary data or distribution advantage, and a UX that makes AI useful without making the user think about the model. The bet is that durable AI value capture happens at the application layer where the customer relationship lives, not at the model layer.

## Why now
1. **Models are good enough to ship real products** — past the demo-only era; now capable of revenue-generating workflows.
2. **Enterprise budget reallocation** — line items moving from "software" generally to "AI software" specifically.
3. **Vertical-AI emergence** — legal, medical, finance, real estate, customer support all getting purpose-built AI products.
4. **Distribution channels open** — enterprises buying, consumers downloading; PMF is finally achievable.

## Core sub-areas
1. **Vertical AI** — purpose-built for one industry (Harvey for legal, Hippocratic for healthcare, etc.).
2. **AI-native productivity** — replacing horizontal SaaS workflows.
3. **AI agents for specific tasks** — sales, support, scheduling, research.
4. **Creator / generative consumer apps** — image, video, music, character.
5. **AI for personal life** — companions, coaches, education.

## Common founder profile
- Domain expert + AI engineer pairing.
- Has direct experience with the workflow they're rebuilding (ex-lawyer building legal AI, etc.).
- Distribution-aware — has a clear bottoms-up or top-down GTM hypothesis.

## Anti-patterns
- **Generic chatbot** for an industry that already has good software.
- **Pure model wrapper** with no proprietary data, distribution, or workflow lock-in.
- **"We'll figure out the wedge later"** — without a specific wedge, the app layer is too crowded.

## Open questions
1. **Will agentic workflows collapse SaaS categories** — does an "AI sales rep" eat Salesforce, or live alongside?
2. **Where do consumers actually want AI in their lives** — companions, productivity, search?
3. **Will the foundation labs (OpenAI, Anthropic) eat the application layer** by adding more features to their main products?
