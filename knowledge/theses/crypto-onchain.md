# Crypto / Onchain

## The thesis in one paragraph
Crypto / onchain is the **third era of the internet** (Read → Read-Write → Read-Write-Own). The premise: protocols that enable user ownership of digital property, identity, and value flows will become foundational infrastructure for the next decade of computing. a16z crypto operates as a semi-independent practice with its own fund (currently in $7B+ AUM range across multiple vehicles) led by Chris Dixon. The horizon is 10-20 years; bear markets are accepted as part of the cycle.

## Why now
1. **Infrastructure maturation** — L1s (Ethereum, Solana, Aptos), L2s (Optimism, Arbitrum, Base) are production-grade.
2. **Stablecoin adoption** — stablecoins are now meaningful payment rails globally; PayPal launched PYUSD; Stripe re-entered crypto.
3. **Regulatory clarity emerging** — post-2024 election, US regulatory hostility has eased; SAB 121 rescinded; clearer paths for institutional adoption.
4. **Onchain economic activity** — DeFi TVL, NFT trading volume, and onchain games have proven non-speculative use cases (slowly).
5. **AI + crypto convergence** — agentic AI economies need permissionless payment rails and ownership primitives; crypto provides them.

## Common founder profile
- Technical, often protocol-engineer-level.
- Long-term oriented; cycles-aware.
- Open-source first; sometimes uncomfortable with traditional VC norms (some founders refuse equity altogether and only take tokens).
- Often anonymous or pseudonymous in early stages (a16z is unusually comfortable with this).

## Anti-patterns
- **"Tokens for nothing"** — projects whose token has no functional role.
- **VC-extractive token unlocks** — bad PR + bad alignment.
- **Web2 nostalgia rebuilds** — "Twitter but onchain" without a credible reason it works better.
- **Speculative meme-coin plays** — out of scope.
- **Founders unwilling to ship through bear markets** — bear-market behavior is a major signal.

## Open questions
1. **Will mainstream consumer crypto adoption happen** — or will crypto remain a developer/finance niche?
2. **Will AI + crypto converge in a real way** — agent economies, AI provenance — or remain separate?
3. **How does regulation evolve under different US administrations** — a16z bet on Trump-era favorable regulation, but mid-term elections + Fed policy could shift.
4. **L1 wars** — Ethereum vs. Solana vs. emerging L1s. a16z has spread bets; no clear winner.
