# Enterprise / Infrastructure

## The thesis in one paragraph
Enterprise infrastructure powers how businesses operate digitally. The thesis covers data, security, networking, devtools, observability, AI-infra-for-enterprise, vertical SaaS, and the next-generation of platforms enterprises build on. The dominant pattern: open source + cloud + AI is restacking the entire enterprise software market over a decade-long cycle. The picks-and-shovels of the digital economy.

## Why now
1. **AI restacks the enterprise software stack** — every category from CRM to ERP to ITSM is being rewritten.
2. **Cloud-cost rationalization** — Casado's "Cost of Cloud" essay opened a debate that's now mainstream; companies are repatriating workloads.
3. **Open source as default GTM motion** — modern enterprise infra companies launch via OSS and monetize via cloud-hosted product.
4. **Data sovereignty / regional compliance** — driving multi-cloud, edge, and on-prem renaissance.

## Common founder profile
- Technical founder (ex-Google infra, ex-Meta, ex-Stripe, ex-Databricks).
- Has experienced the pain point firsthand inside a large tech company.
- OSS-savvy — knows how to seed community before monetizing.

## Anti-patterns
- **OSS without a monetization path** — popular project, zero revenue.
- **"Replace [established player] with cheaper version"** — pricing wars rarely build durable companies.
- **Vertical SaaS without insider domain knowledge** — outsider operators frequently misread the workflow.

## Open questions

2. **Cloud repatriation** — is it a real trend or a niche?
3. **Open source monetization endgame** — does Casado's "OSS is distribution" rule keep holding?
