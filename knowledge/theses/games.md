# Games

## The thesis in one paragraph
Games are the leading edge of consumer technology. Game-engine technology, virtual worlds, AI-driven NPCs and procedural content, creator-led game economies, and the convergence of games with social/entertainment/work make games a venture-scale category in their own right. a16z Games (launched 2022) funds studios, infrastructure, and platforms across web2, web3, and emerging mediums.

## Why now
1. **AI-generated content** is reshaping development cost structure — small studios can produce AAA content.
2. **Creator-led game economies** — UGC games (Roblox, Fortnite Creative) and modding ecosystems are creating new business models.
3. **Virtual worlds + social** — gaming-as-platform extends into entertainment, work, education.
4. **Hardware accessibility** — VR/AR maturation, cloud gaming reducing device dependency.
5. **Mobile gaming continues to dominate global gaming revenue** — Asia/India markets growing.

## Core sub-areas
1. **Game studios** — funding new IP and creators.
2. **Game infrastructure** — engines, tooling, monetization platforms, anti-cheat, hosting.
3. **Creator-led game economies** — UGC platforms, mods, custom worlds.
4. **AI for games** — NPCs, procedural content, AI character platforms.
5. **Web3 games** — true ownership of in-game assets.

## Notable portfolio
- **Roblox** (early backer; consumer flagship).
- **Various game studios from a16z Games Fund One.**
- **AI character / virtual companion plays.**

## Common founder profile
- Game industry operators (ex-Riot, ex-Blizzard, ex-Epic, ex-Roblox, ex-Supercell).
- Or technical AI/ML founders pivoting into games.
- Designer + engineer co-founder pairings.

## Anti-patterns
- **Pure web3 token-first games** without a fun-first design hypothesis (most 2021-2022 web3 games failed this way).
- **VC-built "game-as-a-service" plays** without a true gamemaker founder.

## Open questions
1. **Does AI-generated content commoditize game development** or unlock indie creators?
2. **Will virtual worlds become the next major social platform** or stay genre entertainment?
