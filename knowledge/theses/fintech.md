# Fintech

## The thesis in one paragraph
Fintech infrastructure and applications transform how money moves, gets stored, gets lent, gets invested. a16z's fintech practice covers banking infrastructure, payments, embedded finance, stablecoins (overlaps with crypto), wealth management, insurance, and emerging-market financial inclusion. The unifying frame: software is eating finance, and the legacy financial services stack still has decades of replacement ahead.

## Why now
1. **Stablecoins becoming mainstream rails** — overlap with crypto thesis.
2. **Embedded finance** — every consumer/SMB software adds a financial layer.
3. **Emerging-market mobile-first finance** — India UPI, Latam Nu Holdings model proven.
4. **AI underwriting** — credit, fraud, insurance reshaped by ML.
5. **Cross-border money movement** still expensive and slow — major opportunity.

## Common founder profile
- Operator from inside a financial institution (ex-Goldman, ex-Stripe, ex-bank-engineering).
- Or a software founder who deeply understands a vertical's financial workflow.
- Comfortable with regulation — has a compliance plan, not just a "we'll figure it out."

## Anti-patterns
- **Neobank without a wedge** — undifferentiated consumer banking is dead.
- **Crypto-fintech without regulatory plan** — bank-equivalent risks.
- **"Plaid for X"** — feature companies, not platform companies.

## Open questions
