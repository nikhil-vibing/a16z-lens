# Vijay Pande

## Role at a16z
General Partner. Founding partner of **a16z bio + health** (started 2015). The intellectual voice of the firm's bio/health thesis — increasingly an AI-bio thesis as the two fields converge.

## Background (pre-a16z)
- Stanford professor of chemistry, structural biology, computer science.
- Founded **Folding@home** (distributed-computing protein folding project, 2000) — one of the largest distributed-computing projects ever; foundational to computational biology.
- Joined a16z in 2015 to launch the firm's first bio fund.

## Investment focus / thesis areas
- **Bio + health, especially at the intersection of AI and biology.**
- **Computational biology, drug discovery, digital therapeutics.**
- **Diagnostics, health-tech infrastructure, healthcare AI.**

## Signature frameworks & sayings

- **"Biology is becoming engineering"** — Pande's foundational frame. With CRISPR, AlphaFold, synthetic biology, and ML-driven drug discovery, biology has crossed from natural-science-only into engineerable. This is the inflection a16z bio is positioned for.
- **"The AI + bio stack"** — Pande's framing of the bio company stack: data layer, model layer, lab automation layer, clinical/regulatory layer. Investors should understand which layer a company plays in.
- **"Bio's iPhone moment"** — frequent claim that bio is where mobile was in 2007 — about to be transformed by compute + ML + new tooling.
- **"Software-defined medicine"** — digital therapeutics, AI-powered diagnostics, personalized care; medicine increasingly delivered as software.

## Notable portfolio investments
- **Insitro** (AI-driven drug discovery, Daphne Koller)
- **Devoted Health** (Medicare Advantage)
- **Adagene, Bicycle Therapeutics, others in drug discovery**
- **Numerous AI-bio bets in the 2023-2026 surge.**

## Public writing / podcasts / talks
- **a16z bio essays** — "Biology is becoming engineering," "The case for AI-first biotech," series on AI + drug discovery.
- **a16z Bio Eats World Podcast** — long-running, Pande hosts or guests frequently.
- **Twitter/X as @VijayPande** — measured, scientist-tone.

## Voice/style notes
- Scientist-as-investor voice. Will explain biology and ML simultaneously; bridges the two cultures.
- Doesn't get pulled into culture-war discourse.
- Long-arc patient — biology cycles are slower than software cycles; he's comfortable with that.
- Strong on regulatory pragmatism — doesn't reflexively bash FDA the way some firms do; sees regulatory mastery as a competitive moat.

## What he looks for in bio founders
1. **Genuine technical depth** — scientific founders, not just "MBA pivoting into bio."
2. **AI/ML fluency** — increasingly required; the days of pure wet-lab biology getting venture funding are narrowing.
3. **Capital efficiency for biotech** — bio is capital-hungry; founders should know how to stage milestones.
4. **Regulatory strategy** — FDA / EMA / payer plan from day one, not as an afterthought.
