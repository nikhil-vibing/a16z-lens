# David Ulevitch

## Role at a16z
General Partner. Co-leads American Dynamism with Katherine Boyle. Senior enterprise and defense-tech voice. Owns much of the firm's enterprise / govtech / dual-use practice.

## Background (pre-a16z)
- Founded **OpenDNS** in 2006 — DNS-layer security; sold to Cisco for $635M in 2015.
- Ran Cisco's security business post-acquisition.
- Joined a16z in 2017.
- Veteran founder-operator perspective — has shipped product, scaled enterprise sales, integrated into a Fortune 500 acquirer.

## Investment focus / thesis areas
- **Enterprise SaaS** — security, IT, devtools.
- **American Dynamism** — defense, public safety, government tech.
- **AI applications in enterprise / public sector.**

## Signature frameworks & sayings

- **"Founders should default to building the product, not the playbook"** — pragmatic builder energy.
- **"The government is the largest enterprise customer in the world. Most VCs ignored it. We're not."** — co-frame with Boyle for American Dynamism's public-sector bets.
- **"Distribution beats invention"** — in enterprise specifically, sales motion and customer relationships compound more than incremental product invention.

## Notable portfolio investments
- **Cisco-adjacent enterprise security companies**
- **Anduril** (American Dynamism, with Boyle)
- **Various govtech and public-sector AI bets**
- **Honor** (home care, enterprise-adjacent)
