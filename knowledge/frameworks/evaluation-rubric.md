# a16z Evaluation Rubric — Composite

This is the consolidated checklist agents should apply when evaluating any company, profile, or pitch. Built from blended patterns across Marc, Ben, Dixon, Boyle, Casado, Chen, Pande, Ulevitch.

## The 10 questions every memo must answer

### 1. Thesis fit (0-10)
Which a16z thesis does this map to? Is the fit strong (specific thesis, not "consumer" generally), and is the timing right within that thesis's arc?

### 2. Founder-market fit (0-10)
Why THESE founders for THIS problem? Specific signals:
- Have they worked on this problem before?
- Do they have unfair information about the market?
- Will they keep going through 5-7 years of struggle?
- Can they recruit world-class people on narrative alone?

### 3. Wedge (0-10)
What's the entry point that's small enough to dominate quickly and big enough to expand from? "Boil the ocean" pitches fail this test.

### 4. Why now (0-10)
What's the inflection — tech, regulatory, cultural — that makes 2026 the right moment? "It's a big market" is not why-now.

### 5. Moat trajectory (0-10)
At year 5, what's the durable defense? Network effects, data flywheel, distribution lock-in, regulatory capture, vertical workflow embedding, brand. Be specific.

### 6. Distribution / GTM (0-10)
How do customers find this and decide to buy? Bottoms-up dev, enterprise top-down, consumer viral, community-led, channel partner, paid acquisition. The motion has to be credible and scalable.

### 7. Unit economics (0-10)
For AI: COGS-per-query, gross margin trajectory, foundation-model dependency.
For SaaS: NRR, payback period, LTV/CAC.
For consumer: retention curves, time-to-monetization.
For marketplace: take rate, supply density, transaction velocity.
For hard tech: capital efficiency, milestone-staging.

### 8. Capital efficiency (0-10)
Can this reach venture-scale outcome without infinite capital? Or is it a fund-killer that needs $5B before profitability with no clear exit?

### 9. Narrative power (0-10)
Can the founder tell the story? Will media/X/podcasts amplify it? Will great hires join based on the story?

### 10. The "would I want to spend Saturday emails with this founder" test (subjective)
Ben Horowitz's intuition test: long-term partnership is the actual product venture investors sell. If the founder is impossible to work with, no thesis fit saves it.

## Verdict thresholds (rough heuristic)

| Average score | Verdict |
|---|---|
| < 5 | PASS |
| 5 - 6.5 | TRACK |
| 6.5 - 8 | PURSUE |
| > 8 | HOT PURSUE |

Adjust for category-specific weighting. AI/crypto deals weight unit economics higher; American Dynamism deals weight founder-market fit + mission alignment higher.

## Red flags that trigger immediate PASS regardless of score

- Founder hostility to a16z worldview / Marc / Casado / Ben (rare but happens).
- Business model depends on regulatory capture against startups.
- "GPT wrapper" with no data/distribution moat.
- Founder won't take operational role / wants to step back to vision.
- Cap table is broken (founders < 50% post-Series A, weird investor cleanup needed).
- Co-founder dispute or recent firings of key technical leadership.
- Hiding traction numbers / playing fast and loose with metrics.
