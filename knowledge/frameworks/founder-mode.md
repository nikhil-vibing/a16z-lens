# Founder Mode

## Origin
Paul Graham essay, September 2024. Grew out of Brian Chesky's COVID-era restructuring of Airbnb. Quickly adopted as a16z canonical framing for founder-led companies.

## The argument
Founders run companies fundamentally differently than professional managers. The standard MBA advice — "hire great people and delegate" — actively damages many founder-led companies. Great founders:
- Engage deeply at every level ("skip-level meetings"), not just top-level direct reports.
- Maintain product taste and direction personally.
- Reject the advice that they should "scale themselves out" of the company.
- Run the company more like a unit-led organism than a managerial hierarchy.

## Why a16z embraced it
- Validated Marc & Ben's long-running thesis that founder-CEOs outperform professional management.
- Aligns with the firm's "back founders for the long haul" brand.
- Provides intellectual cover for opposing board-imposed adult-supervision of founders.

## How to apply it as an evaluator
- Strong signal: founder who is engaged at deep operational levels and treats this as core to the role, not a temporary phase.
- Weak signal: founder who plans to "step back to vision" and hand operations to professional management.
- Ask: "Is this founder running this company, or are they running an executive team that runs the company?"
- The first is venture-scale upside; the second is usually below the bar.

## Patterns of "founder mode" companies
- CEO writes / talks at length about product direction publicly (Chesky, Tobi Lütke, Brian Armstrong, Patrick Collison).
- Compensation philosophy is unusual (often lower than market for senior IC roles relative to peers).
- Org chart is unusual — flatter, more functional, less divisional.
- Hiring bar at senior levels is brutally high; many "senior hires" don't last.

## Limitations / risks
- Founder mode can shade into founder narcissism — bad founders who think they're Steve Jobs but aren't.
- Some companies genuinely need professional management at scale (Stripe at 5,000+ employees probably can't run pure founder mode).
- Wartime founder mode and peacetime founder mode look different — see [[wartime-vs-peacetime-ceo]].
