# knowledge/

The long-term brain of this workspace. Everything in here is canonical reference material the agents pull from when evaluating profiles, answering questions, or writing memos.

## Subdirectories

- `founders/` — profiles of Marc, Ben, and every a16z GP whose voice matters. Source of truth for "how would Casado argue this?"
- `theses/` — a16z's published investment theses: American Dynamism, AI, Crypto, Bio, Games, Little Tech, Enterprise, Consumer, Fintech.
- `portfolio/` — notable portfolio companies with WHY a16z backed them and which thesis they sit inside.
- `frameworks/` — decision frameworks, evaluation rubrics, signature a16z mental models.

## Maintenance

1. **Every doc has front-matter** with `type`, `slug`, `last_updated`, `confidence`, `sources`.
2. **Cross-link liberally.** A partner profile should link to theses they champion and portfolio bets they led.
3. **Date-stamp.** Theses evolve. The 2022 American Dynamism thesis is different from the 2026 one. Mark when info was current.
4. **No phantom quotes.** Verbatim quotes need a URL. Paraphrased framings should say "paraphrased."
5. **The `a16z-researcher` agent owns this directory.** When new topics surface, dispatch the researcher to populate or update.
