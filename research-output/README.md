# research-output/

Generated artifacts live here. Memos, evaluations, partner-voice answers, deep-dive reports.

## Naming convention

`YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>-<type>.md`

Types:
- `memo` — investment memo from `/evaluate` or `/memo`
- `thesis` — thesis-fit map from `/thesis-check`
- `partner` — partner-voice answer from `/ask`
- `dive` — deep-dive research from `/deep-dive`

## Keep or prune?

This directory grows fast. Periodically:
- Promote durable findings into `knowledge/` (especially partner profiles, theses, frameworks).
- Archive or delete outputs that were just one-shot exploration.

The `knowledge/` directory is the long-term brain. `research-output/` is the workshop floor.
